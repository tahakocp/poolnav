poolnav

Two robots, same pool, same start, same sensor noise. The controller on each side is the real firmware code running as WebAssembly.

Simulation only. Speeds, slip and gyro noise are round numbers in the range of consumer robots, not measurements of one product. Teal is cleaned, grey is not yet reached. Blue trail is the path; the orange bar is the front of the body.

Source, bench numbers and the firmware size: github.com/tahakocp/poolnav.
